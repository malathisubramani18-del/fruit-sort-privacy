Fruit Sort

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Summary

Fruit Sort does not collect, share or sell your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in and no advertising in this version. Your game progress is saved only on your own device.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Fruit Sort: puzzle game, published by [SVM Games]. It explains what information the game handles and your choices.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game does not ask for or access:

  • your name, email address or phone number
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone
  • advertising identifiers

We do not run our own servers for the game, and the game does not send your gameplay or personal data to us.

3. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you stopped, the game saves a small file on your device with your progress: current level, coins, gems, stars, unlocked levels and your sound settings. This file stays on your device and is not sent anywhere. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

4. Permissions

The app declares basic Android network permissions (internet access and network state). They come with the game engine. In this version they are not used to send your personal information to us or to anyone else.

5. Advertising and third-party services

This version of the game does not show ads and does not use third-party analytics or tracking services. If we add advertising or other third-party services in a future update (for example Google AdMob), we will update this policy before that update is released, explaining what data those services may collect, why, and how you can control it.

6. Children

Fruit Sort is a casual puzzle game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Your choices and data deletion

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You can remove all game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

8. Security

Your saved progress is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot normally access.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The newest version is always published on this page, and the "Last updated" date at the top will change.
